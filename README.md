# Telecommunication-Customer-Churn-Analysis

## Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard developed using **Power BI** for a telecommunications company. The dashboard provides actionable insights into customer retention, churn behavior, contract patterns, monthly charges, and customer risk segmentation.

The primary objective of this analysis is to identify the major factors contributing to customer churn and help businesses improve customer retention strategies through data-driven decisions.

---

# Dashboard Summary

The dashboard analyzes customer behavior across multiple dimensions including:

* Customer retention status
* Churn percentage
* Contract type analysis
* Tenure-based churn trends
* Monthly charge patterns
* Online security and backup services impact
* Customer risk categorization

# Key Insights

## 1. Customer Churn Rate

The overall churn rate is **26.54%**, which indicates that more than one-fourth of customers have discontinued the service.

### Observation:

* Customer retention remains relatively strong at **73.46%**
* However, the churn percentage is significant enough to require focused retention strategies

---

# Contract Type Analysis

The dashboard reveals that **Month-to-Month contracts** contribute the highest number of churned customers.

| Contract Type  | Churned Customers |
| -------------- | ----------------- |
| Month-to-Month | 1,655             |
| One Year       | 166               |
| Two Year       | 48                |

### Insight:

Customers with shorter contract commitments are significantly more likely to churn compared to long-term contract customers.

### Business Recommendation:

* Encourage customers to switch to yearly plans
* Provide discounts or loyalty rewards for long-term subscriptions

---

# Tenure-Based Churn Analysis

The churn rate decreases as customer tenure increases.

| Tenure Band | Churn Rate |
| ----------- | ---------- |
| 0–6 Months  | 52.94%     |
| 6–12 Months | 35.89%     |
| 12+ Months  | 17.13%     |

### Insight:

New customers are at the highest risk of churn during the initial months.

### Business Recommendation:

* Improve onboarding experience
* Introduce early customer engagement programs
* Provide personalized support for new users

---

# Online Security & Backup Service Analysis

Customers without online security services generate higher monthly charges associated with churn behavior.

### Observation:

* Customers lacking security services show higher churn tendencies
* Security and backup services appear to positively influence retention

### Business Recommendation:

* Bundle security services with subscription plans
* Promote value-added services to increase customer loyalty

---

# Monthly Charges Analysis

The dashboard compares monthly charges between retained and churned customers.

### Observation:

* Churned customers tend to have slightly higher average monthly charges
* Pricing sensitivity may influence customer decisions

### Business Recommendation:

* Re-evaluate pricing models
* Offer customized plans for high-paying customers
* Introduce retention discounts for at-risk customers

---

# Customer Type Revenue Analysis

Revenue contribution is primarily driven by existing customers.

| Customer Type      | Revenue Contribution |
| ------------------ | -------------------- |
| Existing Customers | 0.38M                |
| New Customers      | 0.08M                |

### Insight:

Existing customers remain the most valuable revenue source for the business.

### Business Recommendation:

* Prioritize customer retention strategies
* Develop loyalty programs to increase lifetime value

# Conclusion

This Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard provides valuable insights into customer behavior and churn patterns within the telecommunications industry.

The analysis highlights that:

* Month-to-Month contracts experience the highest churn
* New customers are more likely to leave early
* Long-term customers show stronger retention
* Service quality and value-added features significantly impact customer loyalty

By implementing targeted retention strategies, improving customer onboarding, and optimizing pricing structures, telecommunications companies can reduce churn rates and improve long-term profitability.
